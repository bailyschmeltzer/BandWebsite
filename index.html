About Us

Neon Oblivion brings heavy riffs, big hooks, and cosmic energy to the stage. Mixing the grit of classic metal with the boldness of modern pop, the band thrives on creativity — writing original songs that hit hard and reimagining favorites with their own electrified twist. Intense, playful, and unapologetically loud, Neon Oblivion is here to make heavy music that makes your head bang.
